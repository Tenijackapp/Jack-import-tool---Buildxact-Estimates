3 Formatted output

⚠ schedule rows have no Phase.
⚠ rows have a blank Cost_Category.
⚠ row(s) flagged for review: section headings or separators. Nothing is deleted automatically - review, then remove if needed. (Items with a name are always kept, even at $0.)
⚠ rows have numbers in UOM - these look like unit costs in the wrong column.
⚠ name-only contact(s) (a name, maybe an ABN, but no email or phone/mobile). They go on the "Name Only" tab of the download, not the import. They can be added to JACK later as blank records.
⛔ duplicate email(s) shared across different contacts. JACK rejects these - reconcile before importing.
